Learn how to stay safe from phishing emails

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a type of cyber attack where attackers try to trick you into revealing sensitive information like passwords, credit card numbers, or personal data by pretending to be a legitimate organization.

Phishing emails often look like they come from trusted sources like banks, email providers, or popular websites.

How Does This Extension Work?

The Phishing Detector uses advanced AI algorithms to analyze emails for suspicious characteristics:

  • Sender Analysis: Verifies if the sender's email address is legitimate
  • URL Analysis: Checks for suspicious links and URL shorteners
  • Keyword Detection: Identifies common phishing phrases and urgency tactics
  • Grammar Check: Detects spelling and grammar errors common in phishing emails
  • Pattern Recognition: Identifies known phishing patterns and tactics

How to Use the Extension

  1. Open an email in Gmail, Outlook, or Yahoo Mail
  2. Click the Phishing Detector icon in your browser toolbar
  3. Click "Scan Current Email" to analyze the email
  4. Review the results and warnings
  5. If phishing is detected, do not click any links or download attachments

Understanding the Results

✅ Email Appears Safe

The email passed all security checks and appears to be legitimate.

⚠️ Phishing Detected

The email shows signs of being a phishing attempt. Do not click links or download attachments.

Red Flags to Watch For

  • Urgent requests for personal or financial information
  • Suspicious sender email addresses that don't match the company name
  • Links that don't match the text displayed (hover to check)
  • Spelling and grammar errors
  • Requests to verify account information or reset passwords
  • Threats of account suspension or legal action
  • Offers that seem too good to be true
  • Requests to click links or download files immediately

Best Practices

  • Never click links in emails from unknown senders
  • Always verify the sender's email address carefully
  • Hover over links to see the actual URL before clicking
  • Go directly to the official website instead of clicking email links
  • Never provide passwords or personal information via email
  • Keep your browser and antivirus software updated
  • Use strong, unique passwords for each account
  • Enable two-factor authentication when available
  • Report phishing emails to the company and your email provider

What to Do If You Suspect Phishing

  1. Do not click any links or download attachments
  2. Do not reply to the email
  3. Report the email as phishing to your email provider
  4. Delete the email
  5. If you already clicked a link, change your passwords immediately
  6. Monitor your accounts for suspicious activity
  7. Consider placing a fraud alert with credit bureaus
